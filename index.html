—
2. Manufacturing Tolerance Stackup
D_max = D_land + F_tol | D_min = D_land - F_tol
—
3. Solder Mask & Mask Dam (NSMD vs SMD)
D_mask = D_land + 2×M_clr | W_dam = Pitch - D_mask
—
4. Channel Routability & Escape Traces
W_ch = Pitch - D_copper | Traces = ⌊(W_ch - S_tr)/(W_tr + S_tr)⌋

Engineering Guidelines: NSMD (Non-Solder Mask Defined) is recommended by IPC-7351B and major silicon vendors because copper etching yields tighter dimensional tolerance than solder mask registration, and solder wets the sides of the copper pad to provide strong mechanical joint anchoring and self-centering during reflow. SMD (Solder Mask Defined) pads are used when routing channels are severely constrained, for high-stress package corners, or for non-collapsible ceramic balls (CBGA).
